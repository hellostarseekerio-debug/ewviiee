What's On

There's always something happening at the Vic — from half-price pints to quiz-night glory and the roar of match day.

Every Weekday

Happy Hour

Half-price house pints, wines and selected spirits — from the moment we open until 8 pm, Monday to Friday.

It's the best-value pour in Wan Chai, and the reason the after-work crowd makes us their first stop. Grab a stool at the bar and settle in.

Quiz Night

Our infamous quiz. Teams of up to six, free to enter, and the winning table takes home a bar tab. Arrive early or book ahead — it fills fast.
