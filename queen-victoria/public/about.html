Our Story

A British local in the heart of Hong Kong — and proud of it.

Since 2009

Built on a proper welcome.

The Queen Victoria opened its doors in 2009 with a simple idea: give Wan Chai a real British local — somewhere honest, warm and entirely without pretence.

Fifteen years on, not much has changed, and that's the point. We still pour our Guinness the patient, two-part way. We still raise our pies by hand each morning. And we still believe a pub lives or dies by its welcome — so ours is as generous as the portions.

Homesick expats found a front room here. Locals discovered a sticky toffee pudding worth crossing town for. And somewhere along the way, strangers became regulars and regulars became family.

What We Believe

The house rules.

Honest Food

Proper ingredients, cooked properly. No shortcuts, no fuss — just the classics done well, every time.

A Good Pour

Cellar-cool ales, a patiently-poured stout and over forty gins. We take our drinks seriously so you don't have to.

Everyone's Welcome

Match-day crowds, quiz teams, first dates, Sunday solitude — and well-behaved dogs. There's a seat for everyone.
