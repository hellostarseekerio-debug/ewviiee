Our Story

The local that feels like home — wherever home is.

For over fifteen years The Queen Victoria has been Wan Chai's front room: a wood-panelled retreat where homesick expats, curious locals and after-work crowds gather over honest food and a proper pint.

We pour our Guinness the two-part way, raise our pies by hand, and keep the welcome as generous as the portions. Whether it's match day, quiz night, or a quiet Sunday roast, there's always a seat with your name on it.
