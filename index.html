Founded in Hong Kong on 23 May 2024, Shih Ji Hua Sin is dedicated to education development and talent cultivation. CEO Cici Gao bridges the heritage district of Lai Chi Kok with the digital-first classrooms of the Greater Bay Area.

We believe every learner holds unique potential. Through quality education resources, personalised learning plans and professional support, we help learners build ability, broaden international horizons and open more doors — to further study, careers and personal growth.
